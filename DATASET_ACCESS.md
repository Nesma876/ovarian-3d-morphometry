# Dataset access

The original ultrasound and semantic segmentation objects originate from the FollicleFinder resource and are not redistributed in this candidate. Obtain them only through the source authors' verified access mechanism and under the applicable consent, licence and redistribution terms.

Place source data outside version control and construct a local pseudonymised manifest. Never commit patient identifiers, DICOM UIDs, acquisition dates, private linkage tables or raw DICOM objects.
